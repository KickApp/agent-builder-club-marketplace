# Quanto

Find the clients worth cloning. Type `/quanto`, answer one message about your clients, and
get back every client ranked by what it earns you after the labor to serve it, the places
where the numbers disagree with your gut, your ideal client profile drawn from your own
clients, and, if you ask, where to find more like them. It reads your Kick books, your
practice tools, and your meeting notes; it never changes anything in any of them.

Built by [Anderson Petergeorge, CPA](https://www.quantobooks.com/), co-founder and CEO of
Quanto, for [Agent Builder Club](https://www.agentbuilder.club/builders/anderson-petergeorge/quanto).

```text
/plugin marketplace add KickApp/agent-builder-club-marketplace

```

## What it reads

- Kick, when connected: transaction volume, review backlog, connected accounts, open tasks
  per client, and the condition of each client's books.
- Practice and proposal tools, when connected (Ignition, Karbon, Financial Cents, Canopy,
  TaxDome, Keeper, others): fees, agreed services, time by client.
- Meeting notes and call transcripts: files in the folder, pasted text, or a connected notes
  tool (Ping, Grain, Fathom, Fireflies, Notion).
- Exports you drop in: a fee list, invoices by client, hours by client, a client list.
- You: fees where nothing else has them, a rough hours range per client, your grades, and
  your loaded cost per hour, which is the one number it never guesses.

Nothing above is required. With only a fee list and a few notes it runs; each extra source
sharpens the result and is named on the page.

## The flow

| Turn | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Your client list and the sources found, one scope question, then one message asking what each client pays, how hard each is, which you would clone or fire and why, and four firm facts |
| 2 | One page: where the numbers disagree with your gut, the ranked table with the math and books condition, your ideal client and anti-profile, three questions for the partners |
| 3, if asked | Five or six acquisition moves tied to named clients and stated traits |
| Last | One question, what did this miss or over-flag, and a notes block to paste into your project so next time skips the setup |

Each skill also runs alone: `/interview`, `/evidence`, `/rank`, `/profile`, `/options`.

## What the page stands on

- Fee minus hours times your cost rate. Hours are actual when a time source exists and your
  own range when not; a client is called thin or negative only when the whole range says so.
- No formula turns transactions into hours. Kick volume and books condition sit beside your
  estimate so you can judge it.
- Books condition (clean, fair, messy) is on every card and in every table row, because
  messy books are both a headache and a cost.
- The profile is a contrast with names: what your best share, what your hardest share, and
  the exceptions. Fewer than three examples and the heading says hypothesis.
- Every figure names its source. Client-level results carry "Confidential, firm internal use
  only." Findings are questions for the partners, never directives.
