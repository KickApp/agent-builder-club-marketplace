## What it does

Scans an owner's personal bank and card accounts for likely business-deductible spend, such
as software, home office, professional services, meals, and phone and internet. It estimates
the total by deduction type and produces a shortlist for CPA review.

Say things like "find business expenses I paid from personal accounts" or "build a
deduction shortlist for my CPA".

## Safety

Read-only. Every figure is an estimate for review, not filing advice.
