# Worked examples

Synthetic data. Never quote these figures as live data; every delivered number comes from
the current books.

## Books-only, user names the period

> "Prep me for tomorrow's client meeting with Birch & Co, what moved in the books since March
> and what should I ask about?"

1. Defaults: the user named the period, so the lookback default is skipped. Floor $1,000, tasks
   and health included, one-page executive format, all labeled inline.
2. Connectors: no calendar in the session. Mode is Books-only.
3. Books: one set of books for Birch & Co, accrual basis. Used and stated.
4. Window: "since March" gives March 1 through today. Prior window: the equal-length span
   before March 1.
5. Highlights: revenue up 12%. A $8,200 transfer-clearing balance is called out and excluded
   from the cash read.
6. Anomalies: Software +$3,400 against the prior window clears the $1,000 floor.
7. Health: 18 unreviewed items; 42 missing counterparties; reports ready = Yes. One open task,
   "Map new Amex account".
8. Talking points: six bullets, including the Software jump, the clearing balance, and the Amex
   mapping question.
9. Skipped (no connector): last meeting date from calendar; email thread subjects.

## Calendar connected, no period named

> "client check-in w/ Fernwood Dental at 2, talking points pls"

1. Defaults: 90-day lookback held in reserve, floor $1,000, labeled inline.
2. Connectors: calendar present, email absent. Mode is Books + calendar.
3. Books: two ledgers match. The brief waits while the user picks the cash ledger.
4. Window: calendar shows the last meeting on June 10, so the window runs June 11 through
   today. The 90-day default is not used.
5. Highlights: net income down $2,150; credit-card balance up $4,600, noted as a cash-flow
   driver.
6. Anomalies: two identical $1,275 lab charges two days apart, flagged as a suspected duplicate
   import.
7. Health: opening-balances-ready not exposed by the system, shown as "Not exposed".
8. Talking points: five bullets, including a question on the possible duplicate charge.
9. Skipped (no connector): email thread subjects.
